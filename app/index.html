What it does

  • Unlock — Remove open-password protection when you already know the password.
  • Protect — Add a password with AES encryption to PDFs you own before sharing.
  • Saved passwords — Keep the passwords of PDFs you open often, protected by Face ID (Premium).
  • Library — Keep files handy (Premium).

Processing stays on your device. The app is not for cracking unknown passwords — only for files you are allowed to modify.

20+ PDF tools

  • Merge PDFs, split them, extract pages
  • Organize pages: reorder, rotate, delete, crop margins
  • Compress PDFs for email and uploads
  • Scan paper documents and turn photos into PDF
  • PDF to JPG/PNG and PDF to text
  • Sign with your finger, fill in forms, add text or images
  • Watermark, page numbers, redact, permissions, remove metadata, flatten and OCR (Premium)

Open your PDF from Files (iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and more), a camera scan, Photos, or straight from Mail and other apps with the Share button. No uploads, no account, no watermark on results.
